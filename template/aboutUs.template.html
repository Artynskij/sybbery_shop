Welcome to Gooseberry Goods — where we do the overthinking so you don’t have to

We were tired of “natural” labels that meant absolutely nothing — and brands hiding junk behind pretty fonts and beige packaging.

We wanted a store where you could trust every single product. No 80:20 rule. Just real food that’s actually good for you. So we built our own—obsessively sourced and truly nourishing.
