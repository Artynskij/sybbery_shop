What is Gooseberry?

Christina
3 years ago Updated

Gooseberry is a pioneering online grocer delivering absurdly fresh groceries to families throughout the San Francisco Bay Area and Los Angeles. We believe good food shouldn’t come with a tradeoff — be it your time, the quality you get, or your wallet. We designed a new way to get the best quality food that gives you more time back and puts more money in the hands of the people who making it the right way — all at a price that’s the same or less than the grocery store.
